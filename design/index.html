.design

Designing for code, coding for design

As a digital designer, I enjoy bouncing back-and-forth from VSC to Figma, building flexible, accessible styles in CSS that support dark mode, reduced motion, and responsive layouts.

I tend to do my most effective designing in-browser. I hold a strong understanding of how a brand’s visual styles can be translated into a scalable and adaptive presentation framework (SCSS/CSS). For 10+ years I have collaborated with and advised global teams on the design and build of web applications and digital content for B2B, B2C, data & analytics, and digital marketing. I worked as part of a team that designed and developed the authoring environment using Adobe Experience Manage. In AEM style system, I've mapped out and built a scalable style framework to support multiple brands and themes.

Finally, I'm happy to say that I built this site using Astro. Enjoy some of my work below.
